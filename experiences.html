Sea, trails & slow adventures

Everything here can be arranged with one message to Mustafa. Ask a day ahead; in high season, a few days ahead.

On land

Hills, coast & dust trails

RentalAges 12+Book 12 h ahead

Bicycles

City, hybrid or mountain bikes, serviced regularly, with route ideas to the beaches and the hills.

GuidedDrivers 21+

ATV tour

The rugged land around Suerte on a one- or two-seater ATV, with a local guide. Pickup from Suerte available.

HikingModerate

Kyrenia mountain ridge trail

About 8.4 km near Tatlısu with wide views over the Kyrenia range. A guide on request.

Wellbeing

Relax, slow down, listen to yourself

Sea-facing yoga on the Drift deck, meditation under the pergolas, sun-beds and a book. For more, there are yoga and Pilates classes, gyms and spas a short drive away.

Small workshops in the garden, in the shade of the reed roof. No experience needed, and what you make goes home with you.

Beaches

Hidden gems & long sands

Walking distance

Suerte’s hidden bays

Untouched coves a short walk from the garden. Take the sun-bed bag; Mustafa will show you the way.

Tatlısu

Tatlısu municipal beach

About 160 m of sand between steep cliffs, with a gentle slope into the water; no aqua socks needed. Run by the Tatlısu municipality.

Protected beach

Alagadi turtle bay

A protected nesting beach for sea turtles on the north coast. Visit respectfully, especially during the nesting season.

Day trip east

Karpaz beaches

The long, wild peninsula to the east, home to many birds and some of the most deserted beaches on the island.
