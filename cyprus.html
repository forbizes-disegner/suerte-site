Coming from the south

Crossing, step by step

Rules change from time to time. Last checked in November 2026. If in doubt, message us before you set off.

  • DocumentsCypriot and EU citizens cross with an ID card, everyone else with a passport.
  • Car insuranceInsurance from the south does not cover the north. Buy a short north-side policy at the crossing point.
  • Rental carsMost rental cars from the south are not insured for the north. Check with your rental company before you go.
  • Which crossingFrom Nicosia, Limassol and Paphos: Agios Dometios (Metehan). From Larnaca: Pergamos (Beyarmudu). From Ayia Napa and Protaras: Strovilia (Akyar).
  • MoneyThe north uses the Turkish lira. Cards work in many places. Bring some cash for village shops and the market.
  • Phone & internetEU roaming usually does not apply in the north. Check your plan.
Questions

For guests from the island

Can we stay just one night?

Yes, on many dates. Two nights are better: the first evening goes on arriving and settling in.

Do we need a transfer?

Most guests from Cyprus come by car, and parking on the plot is free. If you fly into Ercan, Mustafa can pick you up.

Which languages does Mustafa speak?

Turkish and English. Messages sent from the Greek version of this site include an English copy.
