The way here

  • Tatlısu (Akanthou), on the coast road east of Kyrenia.
  • Coordinates 35.4076, 33.7609
  • Open the route in Google Maps
  • From the south? Crossing and car insurance

Check-in

  • From 14:00, check-out by 11:00.
  • Self check-in with a lockbox. Mustafa sends you the code on WhatsApp before you arrive.
  • Free parking on the plot.

At the villa

  • Hot tub on the terrace and beach essentials.
  • Kitchen with coffee machine, oven and BBQ grill.
  • Wi-Fi and air conditioning.
  • Bikes, kayak and yoga mats.

House rules

  • Quiet hours 22:00–08:00.
  • Up to 4 guests.
  • Pets on request, kept off the furniture.
  • All house rules

Bring along

  • Swimwear and sandals for the rocky coves.
  • Some Turkish lira for the village market.
  • A car: the nearest sandy beach and a big supermarket are 12 minutes away.
  • A light jacket for the sunset cliffs, 5 minutes on foot.

Need anything?

  • WhatsApp Mustafa: +90 542 860 45 45
  • Breakfast, a boat trip, bikes or a dinner table: ask a day ahead.
