Ask a question
How do we book?

Send your dates on WhatsApp and Mustafa will confirm availability and price, or book instantly on Airbnb or Booking.com.

Can we come from the south of Cyprus?

Yes. Cross at a crossing point with your ID card or passport. Insurance from the south does not cover the north, so buy a north-side car policy at the crossing. Step-by-step guide.

We have booked. What now?

Open I’ve booked on this site, confirm your stay and you get the arrival guide: the way here, self check-in and house rules.

How do we get there from the airport?

Ercan airport is about 40 minutes away and Kyrenia about 35. A private transfer from Ercan, Larnaca or Paphos can be arranged; send your flight number 24 hours ahead.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast with fresh local ingredients is available every day as an option. The kitchen is fully equipped if you’d rather cook.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. The second bedroom has bunk beds, and there is a whole garden to explore. Children must be supervised near the water.
